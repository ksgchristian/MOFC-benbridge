About this demo
  • Every person, address and number here is made up. Nothing is submitted anywhere.
  • New lines are translated live by Claude, on your own Claude account, using the approved term list. Before Claude connects, only the preset phrases translate.
  • Form values (names, addresses, income) are never sent for translation; only conversation lines are.
  • Voice input is in the full app. This demo is text only, with a read-aloud button.
