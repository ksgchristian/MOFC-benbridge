About this demo
  • The form is Texas HHS Form 3029-S, “Solicitud de beneficios del programa” (March 2023). The neighbor sees the official Spanish wording for Sections I–IV. The English labels are our reference translation, because this edition of the form is in Spanish.
  • Section V (income) is printed only in English on the form; its Spanish labels are our translation and are marked that way. Sections VI–VII are for office staff and aren't shown.
  • Every person, address and number is made up. Nothing is signed or submitted anywhere.
  • New lines are translated live by Claude on your own Claude account, using the approved term list. Before Claude connects, only the preset phrases translate.
  • Form answers (names, addresses, income) are never sent for translation. Choices are shown from the form's own bilingual options.
  • Voice input is in the full app. This demo is text only, with a read-aloud button.
